description: A homely apple crumble free from hydrogenated fats and refined sugars
yield: 4 servings
ingredients:
- 100 g butter (or coconut oil)
- 1 1/2 cup almond meal
- 1/2 cup flaked almonds or use an extra 1/4 cup of ground almonds
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg (or ginger)
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 kg apple cored and chopped
- 1 tbsp water
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 10 tbsp creme fraiche (or yoghurt)
- 1 1/2 tsp ginger freshly grated
- 1 tsp honey
# ---
directions:
- Preheat the oven to 180C
- Rinse, core and chop the apples into bite-sized pieces.
- Put the chopped apples and a tablespoon of filtered water into a saucepan, cover with a lid and cook gently on a medium
  heat for approximately 8 minutes until softened but still retaining colour and bite.
- Butter the bottom of an 8x8 inch oven proof dish (we use ceramic, enamel or glass) and fill with the cooked apples.
- Use the saucepan to melt the butter or coconut oil.
- Mix the rest of the crumble ingredients in a bowl and then add the melted butter. Mix in with a fork until evenly distributed.
  Chill or freeze the topping if you have time.
- Distribute the topping mixture over the apples and bake until golden (20-30 minutes).
- Meanwhile mix the ingredients for the ginger cream, keep cool and serve with the hot crumble.
