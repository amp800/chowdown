description: These delightful no-bake energy balls offer a quick and healthy boost, perfect for a snack or a pre/post-workout
  treat. Made with a simple blend of creamy cashews, Medjool dates, rich cocoa powder, and a touch of coconut oil, they come
  together effortlessly in a food processor. Store them in the fridge for a convenient grab-and-go option.
yield: 10 servings
prep_time: '5'
total_time: '35'
ingredients:
- 1 cup cashews unsalted
- 6 dates pitted
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 2 tbsp desiccated coconut shredded
# ---
directions:
- In a food processor, combine the cashews, dates, cocoa powder, and coconut oil. Pulse a few times to combine, then puree
  until a large ball begins to form, about 30 seconds.
- Remove the dough from the food processor and divide into 10 equal-sized balls.
- Sprinkle the shredded coconut on a rimmed plate. Roll half the balls in the coconut and leave the other half bare. Place
  in an airtight container and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
