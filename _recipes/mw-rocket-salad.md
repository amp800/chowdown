tags:

yield: 1 bowl
ingredients:
- rocket enough to fill the bowl
- 1 cup hazelnuts roasted
- 1 packet Serrano ham
- 1 pear sliced
- 'DRESSING:'
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp maple syrup
- 1/2 lemon juiced
- 1/2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1/2 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 tsp rosemary finely chopped, optional
- salt to taste
- pepper to taste
# ---
directions:
- 'Serrano Ham: Arrange slices in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake at 180°C for 10-15 minutes until
  dry and crispy.'
- 'Hazelnuts: Roast at 180°C for 10–15 minutes until fragrant and the skins have blistered. Rub them in a tea towel while
  warm to remove the skins.'
- 'Assembly: Combine the rocket, roasted hazelnuts, sliced pear, and crispy ham in a bowl.'
- 'Dressing: Whisk the dressing ingredients together and toss with the salad before serving.'
