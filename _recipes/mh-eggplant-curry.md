description: Buttery-tender eggplant cubes in a creamy and aromatic curry sauce are a winning combination in this easy eggplant
  curry. Serve it with basmati rice or naan bread for a balanced Indian–inspired dinner; it takes about 30 minutes to make.
yield: 4 servings
prep_time: '10'
total_time: '40'
ingredients:
- 2 eggplant cut into 2cm cubes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion chopped
- 2 tsp crushed garlic
- 1 tsp crushed ginger
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cup vegetable stock
- 1 can chickpeas
- 1 can crushed tomato
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 4 piece lemon
- 1 handful coriander
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 4 tbsp Greek yoghurt
# ---
directions:
- Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven.
- Add chopped onion and sauté on medium heat for 3 minutes. Add garlic and ginger and sauté another minute.
- Now lower the heat to low and add curry powder, ground coriander, ground cumin, ground turmeric, chilli flakes, pepper and
  salt.
- Toast the spices for one minute or until you smell their fragrant aroma.
- Add eggplant, vegetable stock, chickpeas, crushed tomatoes and coconut milk. The eggplants should be fully covered in liquid.
- Set the heat to medium, and simmer for 20 minutes. Stir occasionally.
- Add garam masala.
- Stir and simmer for 5 more minutes until desired consistency.
- Top with squeezed lemon or lime juice, a dollop of Greek yogurt, and a pinch fresh coriander.
