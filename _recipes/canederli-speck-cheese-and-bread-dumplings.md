description: Canederli are bread dumplings flavoured with Alpine ingredients such as speck (a local cured meat) and cheese.
  They're best served in a warming beef broth or tossed in brown butter with crispy sage.
total_time: '60'
ingredients:
- 200 gram of stale bread, diced (including crusts)
- 200 milliliter of milk
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 pinch of nutmeg
- 1 knob of butter
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 150 gram of tyrolean speck, diced into lardons (or smoked pancetta)
- 80 gram of emmental, (or local cheeses such as venezza)
- 150 gram of flour
- 1 handful of parsley, chopped
- salt
- pepper
- beef stock, homemade (or very high quality)
- grana padano, grated
# ---
directions:
- Dice the bread into smallish pieces (about 1cm) and combine with the milk. Season with salt, pepper and a pinch of nutmeg
  and leave to soak
- Meanwhile, place a knob of butter in a pan over a medium heat. Once it’s starting to bubble, add the onions and speck. Sweat
  until the onions are soft and the speck is just starting to caramelise
- Add the onion and speck mixture to the bread, along with the beaten eggs, grated Emmental, flour and parsley. Mix together
  then leave to rest in the fridge for 30 minutes
- Divide the mixture into 24 round balls around 2–3cm in size
- Cook in batches in salted boiling water for around 5 minutes
- Serve in bowls of warm beef broth with plenty of grated Grana Padano over the top
