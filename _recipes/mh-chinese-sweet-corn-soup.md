tags:
- mains

yield: 4 -6 people
ingredients:
- '- 1l chicken stock (or vegetable stock)'
- '- 2 cups water'
- '- 2 teaspoons vegeta'
- '- 1 teaspoon sugar'
- '- white pepper'
- '- sesame oil'
- '- 5 tablespoons cornstarch'
- 5 tablespoons cold water
- '- 1 can creamed corn'
- '- 1 can corn kernels (drained)'
- '- 2 eggs, beaten'
# ---
directions:
- Step 1 – Bring chicken stock ingredients to a boil, then turn down to a simmer. - 1L chicken stock - 2 cups water - 2 teaspoons
  Vegeta - 1 teaspoon sugar - white pepper - sesame oil
- Mix cornstarch and water before adding. - 5 tablespoons cornstarch + 5 tablespoons cold water
- Add cans - 1 can creamed corn - 1 can corn kernels (drained)
- Add eggs in a thin trickle while stirring - 2 eggs, beaten
