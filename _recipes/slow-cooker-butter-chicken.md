description: RESTAURANT QAULITY!! You'll never buy butter chicken again.
yield: 4 servings
prep_time: '10'
total_time: '310'
ingredients:
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 (420 milliliter /14-ounce) can light coconut milk
- 1 (175 milliliter /6-ounce) can tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons whole wheat flour
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder, (or more), to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon ginger powder
- salt to taste
- salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1 kilogram boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro leaves (coriander)
# ---
directions:
- Heat olive oil in a pan over medium high heat.
- Add garlic and onion, and cook, stirring for 2-3 minutes.
- Stir in coconut milk, tomato paste, flour, garam masala, curry powder, chili powder and ginger powder until well combined
  and slightly thickened, about 1-2 minutes
- Add salt and pepper, to taste.
- Place chicken into slow cooker.
- Add the sauce and combine.
- Cover and cook on low heat for 5 hours.
- Add the coriander in the last hour
# ---
notes:
- (Optional) stir through 300ml of cream in the last half hour before serving for a creamy consistency.
