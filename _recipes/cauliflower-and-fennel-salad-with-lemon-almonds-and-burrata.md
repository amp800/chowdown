description: “Use hazelnuts if you prefer and regular mozzarella if you can’t find burrata," says Leanne Kitchen.
yield: 4 servings
ingredients:
- 1/2 cup almonds
- 1/2 cauliflower
- 2 bulb fennel trimmed
- 1 handful rocket
- 1 handful basil leaves torn
- 250 g burrata cheese torn into 2 cm pieces
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tbsp dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp honey
- 3 tsp lemon zest finely grated
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 150 ml olive oil
# ---
directions:
- For the dressing, whisk egg yolk, mustard, honey and zest in a bowl until smooth, then stir in the lemon juice. Whisking
  continuously, add olive oil a little at a time (if you add it too quickly the dressing will curdle) until a thick, emulsified
  dressing forms. Season.
- Preheat oven to 180°C. Roast almonds on a baking tray for 15 minutes or until golden. Cool, then coarsely chop.
- Thinly slice cauliflower through its stalk. Cut fennel bulbs in half lengthways and thinly slice. Gently toss cauliflower
  and fennel in a large bowl with rocket, basil and burrata. Drizzle with dressing and toss again. Scatter with the almonds
  to serve.
# ---
notes:
- Use hazelnuts if you prefer and regular mozzarella if you can’t find burrata.
