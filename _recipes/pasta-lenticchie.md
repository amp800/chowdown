description: A hearty and comforting Italian-inspired pasta and lentil dish, featuring tender lentils simmered with aromatic
  vegetables and pasta for a satisfying meal.
yield: 4-6 servings
prep_time: 2-3 hours
cook_time: 1 hour 5 minutes
total_time: 3-4 hours
ingredients:
- 1 Cup green lentils
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion chopped
- 1 celery chopped
- 1 carrot chopped
- 2 litres water
- 2 1/2 cups pasta
- 1/4 can tomatoes
# ---
directions:
- Place lentils in bowl and cover with cold water for 2-3 hours. Rinse and drain.
- Cook onions then add carrot and celery for 5 minutes.
- Add lentils and stir.
- Add water and cook for 1 hour.
- Add salt and pepper and pasta.
