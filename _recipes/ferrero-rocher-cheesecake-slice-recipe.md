tags:

description: This no-bake chocolate slice features layers of biscuits, Nutella cheesecake, melted milk and dark chocolate,
  and a topping of Ferrero Rocher.
yield: 24 servings
prep_time: '385'
total_time: '385'
ingredients:
- 250 gram plain malt biscuits
- 250 gram ferrero rocher
- 125 gram butter, melted, cooled
- 6.00 gm gelatine powder
- 500 gram cream cheese, at room temperature, chopped
- 40.00 gm caster sugar
- 400 gram jar chocolate hazelnut spread
- 125 milliliter (1/2 cup) thickened cream
- 150 gram baking milk chocolate, chopped
- 50 gram baking dark chocolate, chopped
- 9.20 gm vegetable oil
- 120 gram (3/4 cup) roasted hazelnuts, finely chopped
# ---
directions:
- Grease a 20 x 30cm slice pan. Line the base and sides with baking paper, allowing the paper to over the long sides.
- Break biscuits and place in a food processor. Process until finely chopped. Coarsely chop 8 Ferrero Rochers and add to food
  processor with the butter. Pulse until combined then transfer to prepared pan. Press evenly over the base.
- Place 2 tablespoons water in a small microwave-safe bowl. Sprinkle with the gelatine and stir until combined. Microwave
  on High for 15 seconds then stir until gelatine has dissolved. Set aside for 5 minutes to cool.
- Meanwhile, use electric beaters to beat the cream cheese and sugar in a bowl until smooth. Gradually beat in the spread,
  cream and gelatine mixture. Pour over the biscuit base and smooth the surface. Place in the fridge for 2 hours or until
  firm.
- Use a small serrated knife to carefully cut the remaining 12 Ferrero Rochers in half. Place the milk chocolate, dark chocolate
  and oil in a heatproof bowl over a bowl of simmering water (make sure the bowl doesn’t touch the water). Use a metal spoon
  to stir until smooth. Stir in hazelnut. Carefully spread the chocolate mixture over the cheesecake layer. Arrange the halved
  Ferrero Rochers on top. Place in the fridge for 4 hours or overnight to set.
- Remove slice from the fridge 15 minutes before cutting into pieces to serve.
