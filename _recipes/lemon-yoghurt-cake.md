tags:

description: The use of yoghurt in this recipe makes for a fluffier baked cake. A Preshaw favourite!
yield: 10 servings
prep_time: '40'
total_time: '90'
ingredients:
- 125 milliliter (1/2 cup) vegetable oil
- 3 eggs
- 1 tbsp lemon zest
- 2 lemons, juiced, plus 2 teaspoon finely grated lemon zest
- 220 gram (1 cup) caster sugar
- 300 gram (2 cups) self-raising flour, sifted
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 tsp vanilla essence
- 280 gram (1 cup) greek style yoghurt, plus extra, to serve
- 110 gram (1/2 cup) caster sugar
- 80 milliliter (1/3 cup) lemon juice
# ---
directions:
- Preheat oven to 160C. Grease pan ( 7inch round) then line base and side with baking paper.
- Whisk oil, eggs, lemon juice, zest, sugar, vanilla essence and yoghurt until combined.
- Sift flour and mix into mixture with baking powder and salt
- Wisk (hand) until smooth. Pour into tin and bake for 50-55 minutes.
- Let it cool and enjoy! Great served with lemon icing (1 c icing sugar, 1-2tblsp lemon juice), raspberries and candied lemons
# ---
notes:
- If want icing mix 1tbsp lemon juice and 1 cup icing sugar. Then pour over cooled cake.
