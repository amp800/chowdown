description: Simple to make and delicious to eat, this vegan lentil salad is made with fresh ingredients and makes for a really
  versatile side dish. Put on bed of hummus – tomatoes, cucumber and red onion... yum!
yield: 4 servings
prep_time: '10'
total_time: '30'
ingredients:
- 1 cup small green whole lentils
- 1 15 ounce can chickpeas (rinsed and drained)
- 1-2 persian cucumbers (small diced)
- 1/2 cup roasted red peppers (small diced)
- 1/2 cup red onion (small diced)
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley (chopped)
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon zaatar
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1 garlic clove (pressed)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
# ---
directions:
- In a large pot of salted boiling water, place the lentils and cook until tender, about 20 minutes. Once cooked, drain the
  lentils and rinse in cold water to chill. Transfer to a large serving bowl.
- While the lentils are cooking, whisk together the salad dressing ingredients until well combined.
- Add the remaining salad ingredients on top of the lentils.
- When ready to serve, pour the dressing over the salad and gently toss to combine.
# ---
notes:
- 'Storage: The undressed salad will keep well covered in the fridge for 3 days.'
