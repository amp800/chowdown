yield: 8 servings
prep_time: '15'
total_time: '30'
ingredients:
- 500 g pork mince
- 4 eggplant
- vegetable oil
- 6 clove garlic
- 6 spring onion
- 1/2 bunch basil leaves
- 1/2 cup chicken stock
- 2 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 3 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp caster sugar
- 4 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp chilli paste
- white pepper
# ---
directions:
- 'PREP:'
- 1) Eggplant --> zebra-peel, then dice into 1cm cubes.
- 2) Spring onions --> slice into 2cm long batons, and separate into white and green parts
- 3) Garlic --> slice thinly
- 4) Basil --> pull leaves / remove stems
- 5) 'sauce' --> mix all sauce ingredients into a glass jug
- 6) Stock --> organise beforehand (either from packet or vegeta)
- 'COOK EGGPLANT: Heat wok over medium-high heat and pour vegetable oil around the perimeter. Spread the eggplant out evenly
  in the wok, and let sear with a cover on for 2 minutes. Remove the cover, turn the eggplant pieces over, and pour more vegetable
  oil around the perimeter. Cover the wok, and turn heat to medium-low for another 2 minutes until the eggplant is just tender.
  Next, raise the heat to high to cook the eggplant to a golden brown colour, another 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate.'
- 'COOK GREENS & MINCE: With the wok over high heat, pour vegetable oil around the perimeter, then add the garlic, white parts
  of the spring onions, basil, and pork mince. Stir fry for a minute until pork seared.'
- 'SAUCE: Add the eggplant back in, plus pour in the ''sauce'' and stir until everything is combined - about a minute.'
- 'FINAL STEP: Add the green portion of the spring onions. Pour the chicken stock around the perimeter of the wok to deglaze
  it. Stir fry until everything is well-combined, and most of the liquid has been absorbed by other ingredients.'
