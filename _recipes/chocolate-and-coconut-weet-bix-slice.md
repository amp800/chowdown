description: Transform Weet-Bix into this easy chocolate and coconut slice. It makes the perfect recipe for a bake sale, party
  or afternoon tea.
yield: 24 servings
prep_time: '10'
total_time: '25'
ingredients:
- 3 weet-bix biscuits
- 85 gram desiccated coconut
- 120 gram caster sugar
- 150 gram self-raising flour
- 20.00 gm cocoa powder
- 150 gram salted butter
- 4.40 gm vanilla essence
- 375.00 milliliter soft icing mixture
- extra 10.00 gm cocoa powder
- 40.00 gm hot water
# ---
directions:
- Preheat oven to 180°C. Lightly grease a 16x26cm baking pan and line with baking paper. Break up biscuits. Place into a mixing
  bowl with coconut and sugar. Sift over flour and cocoa powder and stir.
- Melt butter in a small saucepan over a low heat. Pour over dry ingredients. Add vanilla. Mix well.
- Spoon mixture into prepared pan and press down to level. Bake for 15 minutes or until cooked.
- Sift icing sugar and cocoa powder into a small bowl. Add water and stir well. Ice slice while still hot. Chill until icing
  is set. Cut into squares to serve.
