description: 'Six ingredients, 30 minutes and dinner for four: it''s really that easy with this colourful salad featuring
  leafy greens, spicy chorizo, bright broccoli and nutty lentils.'
yield: 4 servings
prep_time: '5'
total_time: '30'
ingredients:
- 1 head broccoli
- 200 gram chorizo, cut into 1cm-thick slices
- 270 gram woolworths beetroot & feta salad kit
- 420 gram no-added-salt lentils, drained, rinsed
- 2 tablespoon natural almonds, coarsely chopped
# ---
directions:
- Preheat oven to 200°C/180°C fan-forced. Cut broccoli into small florets. Trim stem and thinly slice.
- Spread florets and stem over base of a large shallow baking dish. Spray with oil. Place chorizo in a single layer on a separate
  small tray or ovenproof dish. Spray with oil. Bake broccoli and chorizo for 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
- Carefully scatter leaves from salad kit over broccoli, reserving feta, walnut and dressing sachets. Return to oven for 2
  minutes. Remove and carefully toss gently until leaves are warmed through and slightly wilted.
- Add lentils and half of the chorizo, half of the feta and half of the walnuts to broccoli mixture. Carefully toss to combine.
  Place dressing in a small bowl. To serve, scatter broccoli with almonds and remaining chorizo, walnuts and feta. Season
  with freshly cracked black pepper. Drizzle with dressing and a little of the oil from chorizo cooking tray.
