yield: 6 servings
prep_time: '5'
ingredients:
- 535 gram plain flour
- 2 teaspoon instant yeast
- 1.25 teaspoon table salt
- 455 milliliters warm tap water
# ---
directions:
- 'Mix Dough: Mix flour, yeast and salt in a large bowl. Add water, then use the handle of a wooden spoon to mix until all
  the flour is incorporated. Dough will be wet and sloppy – not kneadable, but not runny like cake batter. Adjust with more
  water or flour if needed for right consistency.'
- 'Rise: Cover with cling wrap or plate, leave in warm spot for 1 - 2 hours until it doubles in volume, it’s wobbly like jelly
  and the top is bubbly.'
- '[Optional] refrigerate for flavour development for up to 3 days. Remember to take chill out of refrigerated dough before
  baking.'
- Preheat oven, with dutch oven inside, to 230°C (220° fan).
- Using a dough scraper or anything of similar shape (cake server, large knife, spatula), transfer to baking paper.
- 'Dough in pot: Remove piping hot dutch oven from oven. Use paper to place dough into pot, place lid on.'
- Bake 35 minutes covered, then 10 minutes uncovered or until deep golden and crispy.
- Cool on rack for 10 minutes before slicing.
