description: Delightfully crunchy and bursting with lemon flavour, these simple lemon crackle biscuits are perfect for an
  afternoon treat.
yield: 25 servings
prep_time: '30'
ingredients:
- 100g salted butter, chopped
- 1.25 cups plain flour
- 0.25 cup self-raising flour
- 0.25 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1.25 cups caster sugar
- 0.25 cup desiccated coconut
- 2 tbs finely grated lemon zest
- 1 egg
- 0.5 cup icing sugar mixture
# ---
directions:
- Preheat oven to 180°C fan-forced. Line two large baking trays with baking paper. Place butter in a small microwave-safe
  bowl and microwave for 30 seconds or until melted. Set aside for 10 minutes to cool.
- Meanwhile, sift flours and bicarbonate of soda into a large bowl. Add 1 cup caster sugar. Stir in coconut and lemon zest
  until well combined. Add egg to melted butter. Whisk to combine. Add butter mixture to flour mixture. Mix until well combined.
- Place remaining caster sugar in a small bowl. Spread icing sugar on a large plate. Roll level tablespoonfuls of dough into
  balls to make 25 balls. Roll balls, one at a time, in caster sugar to coat lightly, then icing sugar to coat generously.
  Place balls, 5cm apart, on prepared trays. Bake for 12 minutes or until just dry to the touch. (Don’t overcook as biscuits
  will firm up once cooled.) Set biscuits aside on trays for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
