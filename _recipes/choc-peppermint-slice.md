description: Quick and easy decadent dessert
yield: 20 servings
ingredients:
- 2/3 cup (100 gram) self-raising (self-rising) flour, sifted
- 1/4 cup (25 gram) cocoa, sifted
- 1/2 cup (40 gram) desiccated coconut
- 1/4 cup (55 gram) caster (superfine) sugar
- 140 gram unsalted butter, melted
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 3 1/2 cups (480 gram) icing (confectioner’s) sugar, sifted
- 2 1/2 tablespoons boiling water
- 1/2 teaspoon peppermint essence
- 200 gram dark chocolate, chopped
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
# ---
directions:
- Preheat oven to 180ºC (160ºC fan forced). Place the flour, cocoa, coconut, sugar, butter and egg in a bowl and mix well
  to combine.
- Press into the base of a lightly greased 20cm x 30cm tin lined with non-stick baking paper and bake for 12–15 minutes or
  until soft to the touch and cooked through. Set aside to cool completely.
- To make the peppermint filling, place the icing sugar, water and mint essence in a bowl and stir to combine.
- Working quickly, spread over the cooled base and refrigerate for 1 hour or until firm.
- To make the chocolate topping, place the chocolate and oil in a heatproof bowl over a saucepan of simmering water and stir
  until melted and smooth.
- Pour the chocolate over the filling and swirl the tin to coat. Refrigerate for 30 minutes or until firm.
- Cut into squares and keep refrigerated until ready to serve.
# ---
notes:
- 1. Make sure the water is boiling when you add it, this will help the mix to set.
